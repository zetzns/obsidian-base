
|                         Level | Description                                                                                           | Situational example                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|         **1 — A little blue** | A tiny negative feeling that disappears quickly.                                                      | You go to buy your favorite snack in co-working vending and discover it’s sold out.                                                                       |
| **2 — Slight disappointment** | You feel noticeably down, but it barely affects what you’re doing.                                    | You planned to spend the evening with a friend, but they cancel.                                                                                          |
|          **3 — Feeling down** | The sadness stays with you for a while and slightly changes your mood or motivation.                  | You receive a worse grade than you expected after studying hard.                                                                                          |
|             **4 — Dejection** | You feel discouraged and emotionally drained, and you keep thinking about what happened.              | You apply for a job you really wanted and get rejected.                                                                                                   |
|             **5 — Heartache** | The sadness feels personal and painful; concentrating on other things becomes harder.                 | Someone you’ve developed feelings for tells you they don’t feel the same way.                                                                             |
|          **6 — Deep sadness** | The emotion occupies much of your attention and may make you withdraw or cry.                         | A close friend gradually distances themselves from you, and you realize the friendship may be ending.                                                     |
|                **7 — Sorrow** | A strong, persistent sadness connected to a significant loss.                                         | A long-term relationship ends even though you still love the person.                                                                                      |
|                 **8 — Grief** | An intense response to major loss; sadness can come in waves and significantly disrupt everyday life. | Someone close to you dies.                                                                                                                                |
|               **9 — Anguish** | Extremely intense emotional pain that can feel almost physically unbearable.                          | You unexpectedly lose someone you deeply love and struggle to comprehend that they are permanently gone.                                                  |
|              **10 — Despair** | Sadness combined with profound hopelessness—the feeling that nothing can improve or matter anymore.   | After several devastating losses happen close together, a person feels that their future has collapsed and cannot imagine life becoming meaningful again. |



| Emotion                          | Sadness                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Why do we need this emotion?** | To help us process loss, disappointment, or failure and adjust to a changed situation.                                                               |
| **Function**                     | Slows us down, encourages reflection, and signals to others that we may need support.                                                                |
| **What action does it lead to?** | Withdrawing temporarily, seeking comfort or support, reflecting on what happened, crying.                                                            |
| **Facial and body expression**   | Lowered gaze, crying (again), downturned mouth, slumped posture, reduced movement, quieter and slower voice.                                         |
| **Negative outcomes**            | Social withdrawal, loss of motivation, rumination, difficulty concentrating; if intense or prolonged, it can seriously interfere with everyday life. |
