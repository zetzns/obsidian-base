> [!idea]
> Recognising an emotion is not enough.
> 
> After understanding how another person feels, we must decide:
> **How should I respond to their emotion?**

> [!list]
> For example, noticing that someone is angry does not automatically tell us whether we should:
> 
> - argue with them;
> - listen;
> - give them some space;
> - ask questions;
> - calm them down;
> - or leave the situation.
> 

> [!attention]
> The correct response depends on the person and the context.

This is why Ekman considers this fourth skill much more difficult.
