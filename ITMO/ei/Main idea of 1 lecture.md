> [!done]
> Emotions are **necessary, powerful, and evolutionarily useful**. The aim of emotional development is therefore not to suppress emotions but to understand them well enough to have more control over our behaviour.

> [!attention]
> A useful way to summarise Ekman's idea is:
> **Feel the emotion → notice it → create a moment of choice → choose how to act.**
> 

> [!attention]
> Emotional intelligence also involves understanding other people:
> **Recognise another person's emotion → understand the situation → choose a constructive response.**
