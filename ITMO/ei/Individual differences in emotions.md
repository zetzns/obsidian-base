
> [!info] 
> Although humans share the same basic emotions, they do not experience them in exactly the same way.

> [!list]
> People differ in:
> 
> - how strongly they experience emotions;
> - how quickly an emotion begins;
> - how aware they are of their emotions;
> - which emotions they enjoy or avoid;
> - how they behave when emotional.

> [!example] 
> For example, some people avoid dangerous situations, while others enjoy extreme sports because they like overcoming fear and experiencing the **adrenaline rush**.

People can also develop preferences for particular emotional experiences. Ekman himself says that he avoids horror films and violent films because he does not enjoy experiencing fear or anger indirectly. Other people actively seek these experiences.
