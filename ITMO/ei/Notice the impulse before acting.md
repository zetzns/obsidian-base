This is the most difficult skill.

> [!idea]
> When an emotion appears, it produces an impulse to act:
> **event → emotion → impulse → action**
> 
> The aim is to create awareness between the impulse and the action:
> **event → emotion → impulse → awareness → choice → action**

> [!example]
> For example, when someone insults you, you may immediately feel angry and want to insult them back. If you notice this impulse before acting, you can choose whether and how to respond.

> [!attention]
> The key idea is **choice**.
