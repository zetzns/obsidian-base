I mixed up the dates of my presentation for the Optimization Methods course and realized I had completely missed it. I'm used to being a top student, always meeting deadlines and maintaining an excellent reputation among my professors, so seeing my name crossed out on the presentation schedule was so awful.


1. Deep breathing (Body)
I would practice slow diaphragmatic breathing to reduce physical tension, slow my heart rate and calm my nervous system

2. Progressive muscle relaxation (Body)
I would gradually tense and relax different muscle groups to release the tension in my chest and stomach and feel more grounded

3. Cognitive reframing (Mental)
I would remind myself that one missed deadline doesnt erase years of academic achievements

4. Self-compassion (Mental)
Instead of criticizing myself, I would try to treat myself as I would treat a close friend in the same situation
