Ekman compares emotions to **programs running on different hardware**.

> [!def]
> The emotional “programs” are broadly shared by humans, but every person's brain is different. In addition, experience changes the brain throughout life.

> [!theorem] 
> Therefore:
> **same basic emotional system + different brains + different experiences = different emotional responses**
> 
> This explains why even siblings raised in the same family may react very differently to the same situation.
