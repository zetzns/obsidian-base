
> [!nb] 
> Emotions are among the strongest forces influencing human behaviour. They can sometimes be more powerful than basic biological drives such as hunger, sex, and even the instinct for survival.

> [!example]
> For example, a person may refuse food because it causes intense **disgust**, avoid sex because of **fear**, or lose the desire to live because of **despair**.

> [!attention]
> However, emotions are not something we should try to eliminate. They developed through evolution because they helped humans deal quickly with important situations.
> 
> Their main advantage is that they allow us to react **without spending time consciously analysing every situation**.

Examples:

- **Fear** protects us from danger.
- **Disgust** can protect us from poisonous or contaminated food.
- Positive emotions allow us to experience enjoyment and pleasure.

> [!done]
> Therefore, the goal is not to get rid of emotions but to gain **more choice over how we respond to them**.
