> [!caution]
> Sometimes we notice an emotion only after our behaviour has already begun.
> 

> [!example]
> For example, we may realise that:
> 
> - we have raised our voice;
> - we have become aggressive;
> - we have started arguing.

> [!important]
> `Even then, it is still possible to become aware of the emotional reaction and change what happens next.`
> 

This skill is easier than noticing the impulse before the reaction begins.
