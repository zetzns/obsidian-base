> [!attention]
> According to Ekman, we should focus on both:
> - what humans have in common;
> - how humans differ from one another.
> 
> ***We should generally respect individual emotional differences***. However, `this does not mean that every emotional behaviour should simply be accepted`.

Some patterns can be harmful.

> [!example]
> A good example is **explosive anger**. A person may become extremely angry and say insulting or hurtful things. Afterwards, they may sincerely regret their behaviour but not know how to change it.

> [!nb]
> In such cases, the goal should not simply be to “respect the difference,” but to help the person develop healthier ways of responding.
