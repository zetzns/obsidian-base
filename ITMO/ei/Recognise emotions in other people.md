We should learn to identify how other people are feeling.

> [!idea]
> Ideally, we should recognise an emotion **when it is just beginning**, rather than only after it becomes very strong.

> [!list]
> Early recognition can help us notice that someone is becoming:
> 
> - angry;
> - afraid;
> - sad;
> - disgusted;
> - or emotionally distressed.

> [!info]
> Ekman considers this skill relatively easy to learn with proper training.
